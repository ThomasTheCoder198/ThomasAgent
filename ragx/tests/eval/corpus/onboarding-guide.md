# Onboarding guide

## First day

New employees receive a laptop and an access badge from the IT desk on the second floor. Bring a photo ID.

## Accounts

Your email account is created before your first day. The temporary password expires after 24 hours and must be changed.

## Security training

Every employee must finish the security awareness course within 14 days of joining.

## Expenses

Submit expense claims through the finance portal before the 5th of the following month. Receipts are required for any amount above 200,000 VND.
